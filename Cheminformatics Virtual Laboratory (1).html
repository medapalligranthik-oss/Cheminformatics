    📖 Theory

    🔤 What is SMILES?

    SMILES writes a molecule as a line of text. Letters are atoms, = and # are double and triple bonds, parentheses mark branches, and matching digits close rings. Paracetamol is CC(=O)Nc1ccc(O)cc1; lowercase letters mark aromatic atoms.

    🧊 2D and 3D representation

    A 2D drawing shows connectivity, like a map. A 3D model shows how atoms sit in space, which controls how a molecule fits a protein or packs in a crystal.

    🧮 Molecular descriptors

    Descriptors turn structure into numbers: molecular weight (MW), logP (oil vs. water preference), hydrogen-bond donors (HBD) and acceptors (HBA), and topological polar surface area (TPSA).

    💊 Lipinski’s rule of five

    Orally active drugs tend to have MW under 500, logP under 5, no more than 5 donors and no more than 10 acceptors. Molecules that break two or more rules often absorb poorly.
